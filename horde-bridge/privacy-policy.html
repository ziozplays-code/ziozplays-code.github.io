Privacy Policy - Horde Bridge: Zombie Squad

Developer: RAS Gaming · Contact: ziozplays@gmail.com · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Horde Bridge: Zombie Squad (Android package com.hordebridge.game, "the Game") and the services built into it collect, and how that information is used.

1. Information we collect ourselves

The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no social features. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress is stored only on your device: coins, gems, waves reached, upgrades and settings. It is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob and by ad networks that AdMob mediates. These ad partners may automatically collect, from your device:

  • advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked or watched);
  • diagnostics such as crash or performance data.

They use this information to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads. Each partner processes data under its own privacy policy:

You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where the law requires consent, for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for it through Google's consent message. You can change your choice later from the privacy option on the Game's home screen.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. We only receive a confirmation of the item you bought, so that we can deliver it in the Game.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Device information is shared only with the advertising partners listed above, for the purposes described in section 2.

6. Security and retention

Data sent by the ad SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each partner keeps it according to its own retention policy. We do not keep a copy.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, requests about advertising data are handled most quickly through the partners' own tools linked above. You can also email us and we will help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
